# Invoicing and payment

> All listed prices include GST: the customer pays the listed price. Once registered for GST, the GST component comes out of that price and shows as a GST line on the invoice. Confirm registration status and invoice requirements with your accountant. This page is the process, not tax advice.

## How we get paid

| Offer | When | How |
|---|---|---|
| Opportunity audit | 100% upfront | Payment link |
| Builds | 50% to start, 50% on acceptance (default; set per proposal) | Invoice with payment link |
| Monthly support | Monthly in advance | Recurring payment / subscription |

## Every invoice has

Business name, ABN, invoice number, date, client name, what it's for, amount, GST line if registered, due date (7 days default), how to pay.

## Recording money

- Record cash only when it **clears**, not when promised.
- One line per payment: date, client, invoice no., amount, method.
- Keep refunds and costs as separate lines.

## Unpaid invoices

- Due date + 1 day: friendly reminder with the payment link.
- + 7 days: second reminder, phone call.
- + 14 days: pause any further work until it's settled.
