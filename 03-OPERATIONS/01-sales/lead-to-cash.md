# Lead to cash

## Pipeline stages

Track every lead in Todoist (one task per lead, label `lead`) or a simple sheet. A lead only moves when the evidence for the next stage exists.

| Stage | Evidence needed to be here |
|---|---|
| 1. Contact | Real name, how they reached us |
| 2. Need confirmed | They described a real problem and asked for a next step |
| 3. Qualified | We can deliver it, we know who decides, we know their timing |
| 4. Proposal sent | They agreed to receive a written scope and price |
| 5. Accepted | The decision-maker accepted the written terms |
| 6. Paid | Money has cleared (not "promised") |
| 7. Delivered | Acceptance checks passed and recorded |

## Response targets

- New enquiry (form, call, email): reply the **same business day**.
- After discovery call: proposal within **2 business days**, or tell them when.
- No reply: follow up **once** after 3 business days, then let it go.

## First reply (enquiry)

> Thanks for getting in touch. Which recurring task would you most like off your team's plate? A recent example, the tools involved, and roughly how often it happens will help. Then we can have a short call to check the scope. If it's a fit, I'll send a written proposal with the price and what "done" looks like before any work starts.

## Warm outreach

> I'm opening a couple of slots to set up a phone receptionist that answers when you can't, takes the details and texts them to you. Give our own line a ring on 08 7741 4191 to hear it. Is there something that keeps getting stuck between people or tools? If so, happy to have a short chat to see if it's a fit.

Only say a slot is open if there's capacity. Personalise every message. No bulk sends.
