# Discovery call (15 minutes)

Goal: leave with one clearly bounded job, or a clear "not a fit".

1. **One recent example.** "Walk me through the last time this happened. What kicked it off, who did what, where did it stall?"
2. **How often and how long.** Use their numbers and label them as their estimate.
3. **Tools and access.** What systems are involved? Who owns the data? (Never ask for passwords.)
4. **What good looks like.** "If this was sorted, what would be different?" And: "What must a person always stay in charge of?"
5. **Who decides.** Who approves the spend, and when do they decide?
6. **Recap.** Repeat back the one job in one sentence. Agree the next step: proposal, audit, or not a fit.

## After the call (same day)

- Write notes into the lead's Todoist task (or the client folder if they become a client).
- Unsure it's buildable? Say so and offer the audit instead of guessing a build price.
