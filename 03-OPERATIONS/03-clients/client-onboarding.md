# Client onboarding

Starts when the deposit or audit payment clears. Not before.

## Checklist

- [ ] Copy `_client-template/` to the private vault as `04-CLIENTS/[client-name]/`
- [ ] Todoist: one parent task for the client, subtasks from the relevant runbook
- [ ] Send the welcome email (below)
- [ ] Access granted through each tool's own sharing / user invites (never passwords by email or form)
- [ ] Sample data received (de-identified where possible)
- [ ] Kickoff call booked
- [ ] One named contact on their side who can answer questions within 2 business days

## Welcome email

> Thanks [name], payment's come through and we're good to go.
>
> Next steps:
> 1. Pick a time for a [45]-minute kickoff: [booking link or suggest two times]
> 2. Before then, could you add [kevinktg@goodai.au or ops address] as a user on [tools]? Please don't send any passwords; the invite is all we need.
> 3. If you have a couple of real examples of [the job], send them through (remove anything sensitive).
>
> Anything you're unsure about, just reply here.

## Offboarding (when the work ends)

- [ ] Remove our access from every client tool, or confirm the client has
- [ ] Hand over any accounts we created in their name
- [ ] Note the end date in the client folder
