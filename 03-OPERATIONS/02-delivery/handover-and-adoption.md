# Handover and adoption

Getting it working is half the job. Getting the team using it is the other half.

| Stage | What we do | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Build it | Built and tested in their tools (see the runbooks) | All tests pass, client has seen it work |
| 2. Show the team | 30-min walkthrough, one-page "how it runs" doc, name an owner in their business | Owner named, doc delivered |
| 3. Get it used | Check in on day 2 and day 7. Fix rough edges fast | It's been used for real at least 5 times |
| 4. Check it's working | Compare against what they told us was broken at the start | Written note: helping / not helping, with their numbers |
| 5. Make it better | Tidy what's clunky; suggest the next job worth doing | Next step offered (support plan or next build) |

## The one-page "how it runs" doc

1. What it does, in one sentence
2. What triggers it
3. What a person still does
4. What to do if it stops working (who to call, how to switch to manual)
5. Where its settings live and who has access
