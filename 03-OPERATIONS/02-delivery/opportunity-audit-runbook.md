# Opportunity audit runbook

**The offer:** we find the one or two jobs costing the business the most time and hand back a plan to fix them.
**Price:** A$[set by Kev; default proposal A$490, credited against any build that follows]. Paid upfront.
**Time budget:** ~4 hours of our time across 5 business days.

## Steps

| Day | Step | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Payment clears → create client folder from `03-clients/_client-template` | Folder + Todoist task |
| 0 | Send the intake questions (below) + book a 45-min call | Call booked |
| 1-2 | 45-min walkthrough call: their week, their tools, where it sticks | Call notes in `brief.md` |
| 2-3 | Score each job (table below). Pick the top 1-2 | Shortlist |
| 3-4 | Write the audit report (template below) | 2-3 page report |
| 5 | 20-min call to walk them through it. Offer the build as the next step | Report sent, next step agreed |

## Intake questions (send on day 0)

1. What are the three jobs you'd most like to never do again?
2. What tools does the business run on (email, calendar, CRM, accounting, phone)?
3. Roughly how many enquiries / calls / jobs a week?
4. Is there anything that must always stay with a person?

## Scoring each job (1-5 each)

| Job | How often | Time each | Pain if missed | Easy to automate | Risk if it goes wrong (5 = low) | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Pick the highest total that also scores ≥ 3 on "easy to automate".

## Report template

1. **What we looked at** (one paragraph)
2. **The top 1-2 jobs**: what happens now, what we'd change, what stays with a person
3. **What it would take**: tools, rough effort, a price range for the build
4. **What you could do yourself** for free with tools you already have (honest DIY options build trust)
5. **Next step**: the build proposal, or "not worth automating yet" if that's the truth

## Done when

- [ ] Report sent
- [ ] Walkthrough call held
- [ ] Client's answer to "next step" recorded in Todoist
- [ ] Asked: "Can we mention we did an audit for you?" (record yes/no in the client folder)
