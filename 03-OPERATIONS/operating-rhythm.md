# Operating rhythm

Small and fixed, so it runs on a bad-focus day too.

## Daily (10 min, start of day)

1. Check enquiries: contact form inbox, missed calls / voice agent transcripts, email.
2. Reply to every new lead the same business day (see [[01-sales/lead-to-cash]]).
3. Open Todoist "Today". Do the top task before anything else.

## Weekly (Sunday, 15 min)

1. `00-START-HERE`: is "the one thing" done? Write the next one.
2. Pipeline: move each lead to its real stage. Chase anyone waiting more than 3 days, once.
3. Money: any invoice unpaid past due? Send the reminder (see [[04-finance/invoicing-and-payment]]).
4. Empty `99-INBOX`.
5. Pick three Todoist tasks for the week.

## Monthly (first Monday, 30 min)

1. Run [[04-finance/monthly-close]].
2. 30-day check on every live experiment using the kill/scale table below.
3. Review [[06-admin/tools-register]]: cancel anything unused.
4. Pick one item from [[06-admin/automation-backlog]] and automate it.

## 30-day kill / scale check

| Metric | Kill | Iterate | Scale |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enquiries (30d) | < 10 | 10-50 | > 50 |
| Paid conversions | 0% | 1-3% | > 3% |
| Revenue (30d) | $0 | $1-100 | > $100 recurring |
| Clients actually using it after 2 weeks | < 20% | 20-50% | > 50% |
| Monthly churn | > 20% | 10-20% | < 10% |
