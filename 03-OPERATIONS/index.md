## House rules

1. **Tasks live in Todoist** (project "Finish Line" for launch, "Good AI" for the rest). These pages say *how*; Todoist says *what's next*.
2. **Kev approves anything that leaves the building**: proposals, prices, client-facing messages, anything published.
3. **No claim without a receipt.** No invented results, testimonials or savings. Demos are labelled demos.
4. **Never ask a client for a password in email or a form.** Access is granted through the tool's own sharing or a password manager.
5. **Client names stay out of this public repo.** Client folders live in the private vault only.
